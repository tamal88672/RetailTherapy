Affiliate disclosure and privacy

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links to Amazon on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes the price you pay or which products I recommend.

Advertising

This site may show ads from third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

Privacy

You can use this site without an account. If you choose to sign in (with Google or an email link), the sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication service, which keeps your email address under Google's privacy policy. This site itself stores only a random account ID, the username you pick, and your saved lists. It does not store your name, email or photo. A username cannot be changed once chosen and may be shown to other shoppers on lists you share. You can delete your account at any time from the account menu: this removes your saved lists, and the username stays reserved. Third parties such as Amazon and ad networks may set their own cookies when you click their links or view their ads. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

If you tap the heart on a product, your saved list is kept in your browser, and a copy is kept on the site's server. The site also counts which products are saved and clicked. These records use a random ID created in your browser. If you are not signed in, the ID is not linked to your name, email or any account. When you sign in, your saved list moves to your account; the counts above remain anonymous. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes your copy and starts a new random ID.
